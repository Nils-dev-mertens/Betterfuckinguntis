What it does that the WebUntis site doesn't.

  • Works offline

    Syncs once, then every lesson lives on your phone. Underground, airplane mode, dead Wi-Fi — your day is still there.

  • Several classes at once

    Watch multiple classes, even across school years — combined in one grid or filtered with a tap.

  • Make it yours

    Add your own lessons with weekly repeats, hide the slots you skip, color-code subjects, export to ICS or CSV.

  • Never be late

    A Now & Next banner while you're at school, and reminders 5, 10 or 15 minutes before a lesson starts.

On your phone in two minutes.

  1. Step 1 Download the APK Open the file from your downloads or notification shade.
  2. Step 2 Allow the install Android asks once for install unknown apps — allow it for your browser. That's the only hoop.
  3. Step 3 Pick your class Your timetable loads, and future updates arrive inside the app.

No Android phone at hand? Open the web app in your browser — same app, nothing to install.
