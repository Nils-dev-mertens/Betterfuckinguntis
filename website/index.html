Everything the WebUntis site isn't.

  • Works offline

    Syncs once, then every lesson lives on your phone. Underground, airplane mode, dead Wi-Fi — your day is still there.

  • Several classes at once

    Watch multiple classes, even across school years — combined in one grid or filtered with a tap.

  • Make it yours

    Add your own lessons with weekly repeats, hide the slots you skip, color-code subjects, export to ICS or CSV.

  • Never be late

    A Now & Next banner while you're at school, and reminders 5, 10 or 15 minutes before a lesson starts.
